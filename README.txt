PYG — Confession Letter for Yanna
================================

Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

The uploaded song is bundled at:
assets/cherry-flavoured-love-inside.mp3

Click “Play our song” to start/pause the music. The site does not autoplay because browsers commonly block autoplay with sound until the visitor interacts with the page.

Files:
- index.html — page content and your full letter
- style.css — romantic responsive design and animations
- script.js — music controls
- assets/cherry-flavoured-love-inside.mp3 — bundled music

The page includes the butterflies, violets/tulips theme, your “By Luis bading” signature tag, the complete letter, and a closing section.
