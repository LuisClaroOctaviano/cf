a letter from me to you with sincerity ♡
🌷

Dear Yanna,

I’ve been thinking about how to say this properly because I don’t want to rush my words. I just want to be honest with you.

Somewhere along the way, I realized that I genuinely like you.

I know this is probably unexpected, kasi two months pa lang naman tayo nagkakilala. Pero ewan ko ba, somewhere along the way, I started liking you more than I expected. Hindi ko rin naman pinlano na maramdaman ’to, pero I guess some feelings just happen naturally.

At first, I just enjoyed talking to you. Pero habang mas nakikilala kita, mas lalo kong naa-appreciate yung presence mo. I look forward sa mga kwento natin, sa random na tawanan, sa mga asaran, and even sa mga simpleng moments na magkasama tayo.

I like getting to know you more not just yung mga bagay na nakikita ng ibang tao, but also the things you don’t usually share. Yung dreams or goals mo, worries mo, and even yung times na hindi ka okay.

I want you to know na proud ako sa’yo palagi. Kahit may times na things don’t go the way you wanted, hindi nagbabago yung tingin ko sa’yo. Sobrang galing mo pa rin para sa’kin, and I’ll always believe in you bading.

Sabi mo you like butterflies, violets, and tulips, kaya ginawa ko yung poem na ’yon for you. Maybe simple lang siya, pero naging special sa’kin yung mga bagay na ’yon kasi they remind me of you.

Naalala mo nung sinabi ko na goal ko na masanay ka sa’kin? I meant it. Alam kong ayaw mong masanay kasi baka one day mawala or unpredictable yung future. I’ll do my best not to just disappear.

Hindi ko maipapangako kung ano ang mangyayari sa future, pero kaya kong ipakita sa’yo na sincere ako.

I know you can handle things on your own, and I know you’re the type of person na hindi humihingi ng tulong unless you really need it. I respect that about you. Pero let me be there for you sometimes, okay? Hindi dahil iniisip kong hindi mo kaya, but because I genuinely want to make things a little easier for you.

I know you’re strong, pero hindi mo kailangang maging strong palagi kapag kasama mo ako. You can rest, you can be vulnerable, and you can let someone take care of you too. Hayaan mo akong alagaan ka and treat you like the princess you are.

You don’t have to carry everything by yourself. Kapag pagod ka, lean on me. I’ll be here not to take away your strength, but to remind you that you don’t always have to use it.

And kapag may problema ka, may pinagdadaanan ka, or may gusto ka lang ikwento, you can always tell me. Nandito lang ako para makinig sa’yo, kahit gaano pa kaliit or kabigat yung gusto mong sabihin.

Well ayun lang, I like you yanna.

Sincerely, Luis ♡
